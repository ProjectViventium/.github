# Code of Conduct

ProjectViventium repositories are expected to remain respectful, constructive, and safe for contributors and users.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Assume good faith, but focus on facts and evidence.
- Give actionable technical feedback.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or discrimination.
- Doxxing, stalking, or publishing private information.
- Spam, trolling, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Malicious contributions, social engineering, or attempts to bypass security boundaries.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or restrict content and participation that violates these standards.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to ProjectViventium organization owners through GitHub. For security concerns, use the process in `SECURITY.md` instead.
